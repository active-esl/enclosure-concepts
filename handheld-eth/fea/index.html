Ranking only — isotropic PLA — NOT CE certification. Green PASS means the solver finished (often with huge deflection). It does not mean the product is OK for drop, office use, or CE.

You are looking at a relative stiffness ranking of the rear print shell under a few static loads — “does this rib / wall help?” — not a drop test and not certification evidence.

Numbers come from isotropic PLA in gmsh + CalculiX. Real FDM is weaker across layers, so treat this as compare-geometry-A-vs-B, then confirm on the bench.

What green / amber / skip mean

PASS (green)
Solver finished. Amber warnings (defl ≫ 2 mm, σ above yield) are ranking signals — soft geometry under this fiction load — not a product fail gate and not a CE pass.
SKIP (grey)
No mesh nodes in the load region (often the wrong half — e.g. press on an open rear tray). Not a product fail.
FAIL (red)
Mesh / solver / pipeline error. Fix tooling or geometry and re-run. (None on this snapshot.)
